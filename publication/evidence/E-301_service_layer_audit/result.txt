[2] OBSERVED vs DECLARED SPEED -- how wrong is the travel-time weight?
    implied speed from observed run-time  median 53.5 km/h (p10 27.1, p90 73.6)
    declared sectional speed, same edges  median 110.0 km/h
    observed / declared                   median 0.513
    -> the declared speed is a SANCTIONED MAXIMUM. Weighting travel time by it
       understates journey time by roughly a factor of two.
    -> note: the 50 km/h FALLBACK used on edges with no declared speed is
       closer to reality (53.5) than the declared value is.
       The invented constant outperforms the real attribute.

[3] RUN-TIME VARIABILITY -- a congestion signal with no current counterpart
    edges with a p10/p90 band        6,403
    median spread                    4.0 min
    p90 spread                       11.9 min
    edges where p90 > 2x p10         28.8%
    -> on nearly a third of edges the slowest trains take more than double the
       fastest. That heterogeneity is invisible in a single scalar weight.
